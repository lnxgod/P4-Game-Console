Doom Arena compact base v1

This Arena-only IWAD is derived from Freedoom Phase 2 version 0.13.0.
Freedoom project: https://freedoom.github.io/
Pinned upstream release: https://github.com/freedoom/freedoom/releases/tag/v0.13.0

Original Freedoom2 IWAD: 28,787,748 bytes
SHA-256: a8772e088847032510d97ba2312406a6998f21cbab44d4ff10696faa9c0ecd4b
Arena compact IWAD: 12,253,462 bytes
SHA-256: 18dd25d6ed8b719c9d1417324d7ceaaa943af907ae1005e9a36101c0bbe5c1c4

P4 Game Console's compact recipe removes the original campaign geometry and
unused wall texture definitions/patches. All 32 map-name markers remain for
engine lookup. Retained wall texture records remain in their original order;
all retained artwork bytes, sprites, flats, interface graphics, sounds and
music are unchanged. Pure Hades v0.6 and DWANGO5 remain separate unchanged
inputs providing the 29 supported Arena selections.

This file is not a general or campaign IWAD. Keep the original FREEDOOM2.WAD
for ordinary campaign use. The derivative is installed separately as
/GCADOOM/ARENA2.WAD. Both Arena peers need the same complete current bundle;
older content identities and checkpoints are incompatible.
